# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Include:

- a description of the issue and its impact;
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept;
- affected pages, files or contracts.

We will acknowledge the report, investigate, and keep you updated until it is resolved. Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Scope

- This frontend (code in this repository)
- The SorDex smart contracts listed in [README.md](README.md#smart-contracts)

Out of scope: third-party wallets, BNB Chain itself, and tokens listed by third parties.
